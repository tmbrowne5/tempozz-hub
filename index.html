The short version

Keep it chill and we're good.

  1. Be respectful. Disagree all you want, just don't be a jerk about it.
  2. No hate, harassment, or bullying. No slurs, no picking on people, and no pretending it was just a joke.
  3. No spam or scams. No random links, fake giveaways, sketchy downloads, or DM advertising.
  4. Keep private info private. No doxxing, and no sharing someone's DMs, photos, or personal details without their permission.
  5. Keep NSFW out. That goes for messages, names, avatars, and memes.
  6. Use the right channels. Memes in memes, LFG in looking-for-group, and so on.
  7. No politics. We talk games, ideas, and dumb jokes.
  8. Follow Discord's terms (opens in a new tab) and community guidelines (opens in a new tab).
  9. Mods have the final say. If a mod asks you to drop something, drop it.

Break one and it can be a warning, a mute, a kick, or a ban. Questions go to a mod.

Have fun and be cool.
